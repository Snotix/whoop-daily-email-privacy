WHOOP Daily Email Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Information Accessed

With your permission, this application accesses WHOOP recovery, sleep, and cycle data to prepare your daily fitness report.

How Data Is Used

Your data is used to generate and email your daily report to the email address you configure.

Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. Access tokens and credentials should be stored securely.

Data Retention

Only data necessary to provide the report should be retained. Stored data and access tokens should be deleted when no longer needed.

Third-Party Services

This application uses WHOOP to access authorized data and Gmail to deliver reports. Their privacy policies apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the contact email provided in the application settings.

This is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.
